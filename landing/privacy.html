1. Introduction

Rasen Labs Pte. Ltd. (“Rasen”, “we”, “us”) operates Pine AI, an AI front desk that answers calls, calls back new leads and books jobs for home-service businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and share personal information when you visit our websites, fill in our forms, talk to Pine AI, or otherwise communicate with us.

It does not cover third-party websites, information we process on behalf of our business customers about their callers (that is governed by our agreement with that customer, who controls that data), or job applicants.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. Name, phone number, email, business name, trade, city and state, team size, call volume, how you handle calls today, preferred time to call, notes you type, and your consent choices.

Calls and messages. When you ask Pine AI to call you, or call or text us, we and our providers record and transcribe the conversation and keep related metadata (numbers, time, duration). Calls with Pine AI are made by an AI voice agent and you will be told this at the start of the call.

Account and billing. If you become a customer: login details, billing contact and payment information, processed by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers.

Technical and usage data. IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, ad-click identifiers and similar data collected through cookies, pixels and analytics tools.

From others. Advertising platforms (for example a lead form you submitted on Meta), business directories and public sources about your company.

3. How we use your information

  • To call you back, give you a live demo of Pine AI and answer your questions.
  • To set up, run, support and bill for the service, including your trial.
  • To send service messages and, where you agreed, marketing by email, phone or text.
  • To measure and improve our website, ads and AI agents, including reviewing call recordings and transcripts for quality.
  • To keep our services secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.
  • To create de-identified or aggregated data that no longer identifies you.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who act for us: telephony and voice-AI providers, speech-to-text and language-model providers, cloud hosting, CRM and spreadsheets, analytics, email and payment processors. They may use it only to provide services to us.
  • Advertising partners, to measure ad results (for example a conversion event), subject to your cookie choices.
  • Authorities or others where required by law or to protect rights, safety and property.
  • A buyer or successor in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this policy.
  • Anyone else with your consent.

Mobile numbers and text-message opt-in data are never shared with third parties for their own marketing.

5. Data security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls and reputable providers to protect your information. Payment data is handled by PCI-compliant processors. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including under Singapore's PDPA, the GDPR and US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may ask to access, correct, delete or port your information, object to or restrict some processing, or withdraw consent. To do so, email support@rasen.ai. We will verify your request and reply within the time the law requires. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails with the link in each email, reply STOP to any text, or tell us on a call that you don't want further calls.

7. Cookies and tracking

We use strictly necessary cookies to run the site, analytics cookies to understand usage, and advertising pixels (such as the Meta pixel) to measure campaigns. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; some features may not work without them. Where required we ask for consent first, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of targeted advertising.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above: lead and demo records for up to 24 months after our last contact, call recordings and transcripts for up to 12 months unless needed longer for a dispute, and billing records as long as tax law requires. Then we delete or de-identify it.

9. International transfers

Rasen is based in Singapore and our providers may process data in the United States, India and other countries. When we transfer data across borders we use contractual and other safeguards so it gets protection comparable to that of its home country.

10. Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites and platforms. Their privacy practices are their own; please read their policies.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version, and we will give extra notice of material changes where the law requires.
